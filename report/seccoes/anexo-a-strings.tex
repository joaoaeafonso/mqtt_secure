\section{\emph{Strings} de pesquisa por base de dados}
\label{anx:strings}

Pesquisas executadas em \dataPesquisa{}, restringidas a publicações de 2014 a
2026. O ACM Digital Library e a Web of Science não foram pesquisados
(Secção~\ref{sec:string}); a \emph{string} secundária não pôde ser executada no
ScienceDirect por exceder o limite de operadores booleanos. Os grupos G1 a
G3 são os da Tabela~\ref{tab:grupos}; nas \emph{strings} secundárias acrescenta-se,
com \texttt{AND}, o grupo G4.

\newcommand{\str}[1]{\noindent\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}\footnotesize\ttfamily\raggedright #1\end{minipage}}

\subsection*{IEEE Xplore (\emph{Command Search}, campo \emph{All Metadata})}
\str{("All Metadata":"MQTT" OR "All Metadata":"MQTT-SN" OR "All Metadata":"MQ Telemetry Transport"
OR "All Metadata":"Message Queuing Telemetry Transport" OR "All Metadata":"Message Queue Telemetry Transport")
AND ("All Metadata":security OR "All Metadata":secure OR "All Metadata":authentication
OR "All Metadata":authorization OR "All Metadata":"access control" OR "All Metadata":encryption
OR "All Metadata":confidentiality OR "All Metadata":integrity OR "All Metadata":privacy
OR "All Metadata":attack* OR "All Metadata":vulnerabilit* OR "All Metadata":intrusion)
AND ("All Metadata":"Internet of Things" OR "All Metadata":IoT OR "All Metadata":IIoT
OR "All Metadata":"publish/subscribe" OR "All Metadata":"publish-subscribe"
OR "All Metadata":"machine-to-machine" OR "All Metadata":M2M)\\
Filtro: Year 2014--2026; Conferences, Journals.}

\subsection*{Scopus (\emph{Advanced document search})}
\str{TITLE-ABS-KEY(("MQTT" OR "MQTT-SN" OR "MQ Telemetry Transport"
OR "Message Queuing Telemetry Transport" OR "Message Queue Telemetry Transport")
AND ("security" OR "secure" OR "authentication" OR "authorization" OR "access control"
OR "encryption" OR "confidentiality" OR "integrity" OR "privacy" OR attack* OR vulnerabilit*
OR "intrusion")
AND ("Internet of Things" OR "IoT" OR "IIoT" OR "publish/subscribe" OR "publish-subscribe"
OR "machine-to-machine" OR "M2M"))
AND PUBYEAR > 2013 AND PUBYEAR < 2027 AND DOCTYPE(ar OR cp)
AND LANGUAGE(english OR portuguese)\\
Na \emph{string} secundária, \texttt{DOCTYPE(ar OR cp OR re)}, para incluir as revisões.}

\subsection*{ScienceDirect (campo \emph{Title, abstract or author-specified keywords})}
O ScienceDirect não suporta o carácter universal \texttt{*} e limita o número de
operadores booleanos por campo; a \emph{string} foi, por isso, dividida em duas
consultas cujos resultados foram unidos:
\par\medskip
\str{(1) ("MQTT" OR "Message Queuing Telemetry Transport") AND (security OR authentication
OR authorization OR encryption) AND ("Internet of Things" OR IoT)\\[4pt]
(2) ("MQTT" OR "Message Queuing Telemetry Transport") AND (attack OR attacks OR vulnerability
OR intrusion OR privacy) AND ("Internet of Things" OR IoT)\\
Filtro: Years 2014--2026; Article types: Research articles.}

\subsection*{\emph{String} secundária (revisões), no IEEE Xplore e no Scopus}
\str{<\emph{string} principal> AND ("systematic literature" OR "systematic review"
OR "literature review" OR "survey")}
